Ask a question about a country

An open-source language model running on your device works out which of the Studio's analyses your question asks for. The answer — every number — comes from the Studio's validated calculations on its built-in data, with sources, assumptions and limits. The model never writes figures.

If your question names a country, that country is used.

The model is not loaded yet. The first AI question downloads it once (about 23 MB from public open-source hosts: jsDelivr and Hugging Face); after that it is cached. No fee, no account, no API key.

What this assistant does and does not do. It answers questions about regional poverty, observed spending versus need (Brazil), hypothetical funding rules, regional health and education indicators, and rainfall — for any country in the Studio whose data support it. It declines forecasts, questions about policy effects, policy recommendations and unrelated topics, and says so when the data are not available. Experimental; not an official World Bank tool.
