AI Research Assistant

Ask an economic research question in plain language. A language model — Cloudflare Workers AI (your question and the computed facts are sent to Cloudflare) or, optionally, an open model running entirely in your browser — proposes a plan — indicators from the Studio’s catalogue, coverage, sample and supported methods. You check and approve it; the Studio’s validated engine runs it; the model then explains the computed results, and every number it uses is checked against them.
