Prototype, not a policy recommendation. The default dataset contains invented regions and values. Upload a compatible CSV to analyse your own data. All calculations run in your browser; this page does not transmit your file.

1. Evidence inputs

Required columns: region, population, poverty_rate, spending. Poverty rate may be a percentage (0–100), but use the same scale across regions. Spending must use a consistent unit. Optional: service_access (0–100).

2. Funding choices

Important: Allocation by estimated numbers in poverty is a funding rule, not an estimate of the poverty reduction that spending will cause. It ignores local delivery costs, marginal impacts and legal constraints.

3. Take the analysis

Exports include the underlying calculations and caveats. No microdata should be entered into this prototype.

Methods and limitations

Estimated poor population = regional population × poverty rate / 100. Population allocation weights use population shares. Need allocation weights use shares of estimated poor population. The blend combines the two sets of shares. Existing spending per person = input spending / population. A cross-region spending ratio is descriptive and says nothing about marginal programme effectiveness.

Do not combine incompatible years, survey poverty lines, currencies, geographic boundaries or expenditure classifications. The tool does not estimate causal impacts, correct survey-design uncertainty, or harmonise fiscal data automatically. Regional population and survey poverty estimates need matching reference populations. Geographic attribution of national spending may be inappropriate.
