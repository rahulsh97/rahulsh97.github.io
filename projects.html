Research preview · NFHS-4/501

District Change Lab

Compare changes in child health, maternal care and household conditions across 262 matched districts in the opening question. Inspect district estimates, coloured change tiles, a fitted scatter and a within-state regression sensitivity check.

Partial transcription: 341 districts across 21 states/UTs. Source district values still require a fact-sheet spot check. Associations are descriptive, not causal.

Live dashboard · India02

BharatViz

State maps and ranked views of two NFHS-5 indicators—women’s anaemia and schooling—and a separate ASI 2023–24 registered factory-sector view of net value added and wages per worker. Includes an ASI productivity–pay scatter and aggregate CSV export.

NFHS and ASI are separate descriptive layers; the ASI figures are current-price levels, not wage growth or causal effects.

An HTML and JavaScript tool following a research funding portfolio from proposals and reviewer allocation through awards, milestones, disbursements, risks and outputs. Explore a nine-entity synthetic dataset with filters and charts.

Research tool · India (RBI KLEMS) + OECD04

Industry Productivity & Pay Atlas

When industry productivity changes, how much of value added reaches labour—and how much of an aggregate change is within industries versus between them? RBI India KLEMS manufacturing (1980–2023) with an exact, selectable-year within/composition decomposition, plus a separate OECD STAN cross-country plane. Shareable views, plotted-data export and a methods panel.

Descriptive accounting, not causal. RBI’s broad labour-income share (incl. imputed self-employed) is a different measure from OECD employee compensation; the planes are never pooled or subtracted.

Research preview · Green finance05

Who Pays to Green the Supply Chain?

Follow Germany’s motor-vehicle industry to its cross-border supplier sectors using 2022 OECD input accounts. Explore proportionally allocated supplier emissions and value added, then change the assumed cost and buyer contribution to see how an illustrative upgrade burden shifts.

Published sector accounts; modelled first-tier allocations; hypothetical financing. The data do not observe supplier contracts, upgrade costs or loans. Source coverage and exclusions are shown in the tool.

02 / Research infrastructure

India survey packages.

Public R package source code for working with India’s major firm and labour surveys. Development status: the prepared release assets expected by the current download helpers have not been published. These repositories are code previews, not working one-click microdata downloads. Obtain survey data through authorised source channels.
