Industry Productivity & Pay Atlas

When productivity changes, how much of value added reaches labour? Compare industries and separate within-industry shifts from changes in economic composition.

Descriptive accounting, not causal. India and international planes use different labour-income concepts and are not pooled.

OECD ICIO and GHG accounts · Research preview

Who Pays to Green the Supply Chain?

Choose from 3,926 buyer cases across 81 economies and 50 industries. Trace cross-border suppliers, compare visible first-tier emissions with emissions hidden deeper upstream, and test how a hypothetical decarbonisation burden changes when buyers fund part of the upgrade.

Published sector accounts, modelled allocations and user-defined financing scenarios. The tool does not observe firm contracts, loans, upgrade costs or causal effects.

UNIDO INDSTAT Rev.4 · Calibrated screening · Research preview

Industrial Employment Risk Monitor

An open, calibrated early-warning research tool for identifying country–industry pairs at elevated risk of severe manufacturing employment contraction. Screen 94 economies across 24 manufacturing industries by calibrated risk tier, with a plain-language reading of the warning signals behind each flag.

A calibrated screening system, not a forecast, not causal and not policy advice. Employees-only outcome; no nominal monetary levels; weaker for countries absent from training. Out-of-time validated.

Malawi IHS6 2024/25 · Precomputed research preview

Maize Compensation Lab

When maize prices rise, who should a fixed compensation budget reach? Compare an equal per-person transfer, district targeting, proxy means tests and perfect-information benchmarks across 972 validated price and policy scenarios and three welfare objectives, with survey-bootstrap uncertainty and district diagnostics.

Precomputed results from a validated household-survey analysis; nothing is simulated in the browser. Scenario simulations, not forecasts or causal estimates. Proxy-means-test results are upper bounds for a real registry; geography is district level only.
