\section*{Profile}
Applied economist and research portfolio lead working on firms, jobs and institutions in developing economies. Bring seven years of experience across quantitative research, evidence funding, policy engagement and programme delivery. At the International Growth Centre, manage an \pounds8.5 million portfolio of 43 projects across 16 countries, working with researchers, donors and development finance institutions on firm productivity, SME finance and inclusive growth. Build reproducible analytical tools and translate research into decisions, partnerships and public-facing work.
