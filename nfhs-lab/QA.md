# District fact-sheet spot check

The [Government of Assam's NFHS-5 district fact-sheet directory](https://nhm.assam.gov.in/documents-detail/national-family-health-survey-5-2019-20) links to the [official Barpeta fact sheet](https://nhm.assam.gov.in/sites/default/files/swf_utility_folder/departments/nhm_lipl_in_oid_6/menu/document/barpeta_1.pdf). The PDF places NFHS-5 then NFHS-4 values side by side. The app stores values in the reverse order, `[NFHS-4, NFHS-5]`.

| Barpeta measure | PDF NFHS-4 | PDF NFHS-5 | App NFHS-4 | App NFHS-5 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Improved sanitation, p. 2, indicator 9 | 36.3 | 63.7 | 36.3 | 63.7 |
| Clean cooking fuel, p. 2, indicator 10 | 22.6 | 38.7 | 22.6 | 38.7 |
| Women with 10+ years schooling, p. 2, indicator 15 | 23.9 | 31.7 | 23.9 | 31.7 |
| Four or more antenatal visits, p. 3, indicator 33 | 47.5 | 43.6 | 47.5 | 43.6 |
| Institutional births, p. 3, indicator 42 | 51.9 | 87.6 | 51.9 | 87.6 |

These ten cells match the official sheet. This is a **limited spot check**, not validation of all nine measures or all districts. The official PDF service timed out when we requested its later pages and other district sheets. The app remains a research preview; apparent outliers and any finding intended for citation require checking against the relevant official PDF. The data build tests cover structure, missing values and arithmetic, not transcription accuracy.
