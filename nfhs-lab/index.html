05 / READ BEFORE INTERPRETING

Useful exploration.
Careful inference.

Coverage. NFHS-4 (2015–16) and NFHS-5 (2019–21) values come from published district fact sheets via a third-party transcription covering 341 districts in 21 states/UTs. For the opening comparison, only 262 have values in both rounds. This is not all 707 NFHS-5 districts.

Regression. The solid line is ordinary least squares across district averages. “Account for state” compares districts within each state by removing state means. Coefficients show the change in the outcome associated with a 10-percentage-point change in the selected comparison indicator. No individual data, survey weights, sample-size precision or causal identification are available in this compilation. State-level correlation and changing district boundaries may affect the result. No standard errors or significance claims are shown.

Validation status. The compiler says its district transcription received minimal quality checks. Five Barpeta measures in both rounds match the official district sheet, but the full extract has not been reconciled against original PDFs. Treat estimates and apparent outliers as candidates for verification, not definitive findings. NFHS-6 will be added only once district-level comparability and values are checked.
