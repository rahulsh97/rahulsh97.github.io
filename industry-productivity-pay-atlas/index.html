Decompose a change

Pick any two years. The aggregate change splits exactly into a within-industry part and a composition (between-industry) part.

Formula & definitions

St = Σi wit · sit, where w is each industry's share of nominal manufacturing value added and s is its labour-income share of value added.

ΔS = Σi mean(wi)·Δsi (within-industry) + Σi mean(si)·Δwi (composition). Symmetric; the two parts sum exactly to ΔS.

Labour income (RBI KLEMS) = compensation of employees + the labour part of self-employed mixed income (imputed). It is a broad measure, not ASI wages paid to formal workers. This is accounting, not a causal explanation.
