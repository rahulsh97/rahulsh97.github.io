READ THE METHOD

Three distinct kinds of numbers.

1

Observed

OECD ICIO intermediate-input purchases link supplier sectors to a buyer industry in a single year. Aggregate sector accounts, never individual firms.

2

Allocated

Supplier-sector emissions and value added are multiplied by the buyer's share of that sector's gross output. This proportional allocation assumes identical intensity across the sector's buyers; it excludes later upstream tiers.

3

Assumed

A target, cost per tonne and buyer share create a financing scenario. Supplier burden divided by allocated value added is a scale comparison, not a default probability, observed affordability, or causal estimate.
