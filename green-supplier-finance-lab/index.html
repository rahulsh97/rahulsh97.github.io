READ THE METHOD

Three distinct kinds of numbers.

1

Published account

OECD ICIO 2025 intermediate-input purchases link foreign supplier sectors to a buyer industry in 2022, and OECD production-based GHG and value added by sector. Aggregate sector accounts, never individual firms.

2

Modelled allocation

Supplier-sector emissions and value added are multiplied by this buyer's share of that sector's gross output. Proportional first-tier allocation; it assumes identical intensity across a sector's buyers and excludes deeper upstream tiers. It is not a measured Scope 3 footprint.

3

Assumed financing

A target, cost per tonne and buyer share create a financing scenario. Supplier burden divided by allocated value added is a scale comparison, not a default probability, observed affordability, or causal estimate.

This research preview combines published sector-level production accounts with transparent proportional allocation and user-defined financing scenarios. It is intended for policy screening, research exploration and teaching. It is not a firm-level carbon inventory, a credit assessment, a causal model or an investment recommendation. First-tier suppliers only.
