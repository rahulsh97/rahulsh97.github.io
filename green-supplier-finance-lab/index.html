01B / UPSTREAM

How much is hidden upstream?

Direct emissions occur in the industry supplying the buyer. Deeper-upstream emissions occur in industries supplying that supplier and in earlier production stages. The multiplier traces these requirements through the input–output network. “All-tier emissions embodied in purchased inputs” is not necessarily the buyer's complete corporate Scope 3 footprint: OECD units are sectors, and the calculation may exclude final demand, capital formation and downstream emissions.

READ THE METHOD

Three distinct kinds of numbers.

1

Published account

OECD ICIO 2025 intermediate-input purchases link foreign supplier sectors to a buyer industry in 2022, and OECD production-based GHG and value added by sector. Aggregate sector accounts, never individual firms.

2

Modelled allocation

Supplier-sector emissions and value added are multiplied by this buyer's share of that sector's gross output. Proportional first-tier allocation; it assumes identical intensity across a sector's buyers and excludes deeper upstream tiers. It is not a measured Scope 3 footprint.

3

Assumed financing

A target, cost per tonne and buyer share create a financing scenario. Supplier burden divided by allocated value added is a scale comparison, not a default probability, observed affordability, or causal estimate.

Upstream multiplier: method, units, classification & 2020 validation

All-tier multiplier. The technical-coefficient matrix is A = Z · diag(1/x) with A[i,j] = Z[i,j]/xj (Z[i,j] = intermediate deliveries from sector i to sector j; xj = sector j gross output). Direct intensity gi = production emissionsi / xi (t CO₂e per USD million). The all-tier Leontief multiplier is m = g′ (I − A)⁻¹, solved as (I − A)ᵀ m = g (no dense inverse). For a purchase z from first-tier supplier i: direct = gi·z (= the allocated first-tier emissions); all-tier embodied = mi·z; deeper-upstream = (mi − gi)·z ≥ 0; direct visibility = gi/mi ∈ [0,1].

Scope. This is all-tier emissions embodied in purchased inputs, not necessarily the buyer's complete corporate Scope 3 footprint: OECD units are sectors rather than firms, and the calculation may exclude final demand, capital formation and downstream emissions. The direct first-tier figure is the strict-matched exposure; the all-tier figure adds deeper tiers via the network.

Sources & classification (2022). OECD ICIO 2025 Regular 2022_SML.csv and GHG Footprints MAIN PROD_GHG 2022. Emissions match ICIO sectors by an injective concordance (C24A→C241_2431, C24B→C242_2432, C302T309→C30X301; else identity), so no combined sector total is duplicated across sub-sectors. Rest-of-World has no production-emissions series (g = 0), which understates upstream routed through ROW.

2020 validation. The method was reconstructed for 2020 on the matching ICIO (2023 vintage, 77×45) + 2020 PROD_GHG and compared to OECD's official multiplier (ghg_mult_77_7745_2020, definition: total direct+indirect GHG in kt CO₂e per USD million of final demand). Result: the method is exactly reproduced (OECD's multiplier run back through the network returns the independent emissions vector, median ratio 1.00); Spearman rank correlation 0.93; direct visibility share reconciles to ~1 pp on the largest sectors. Absolute levels differ by ~6% median, from production-emissions vintage differences (emission-intensive sectors, small economies) and ROW emissions absent from PROD_GHG. All-tier levels are therefore modelled estimates within this documented tolerance.

Financing stays first-tier. The financing scenario uses only direct first-tier allocated emissions. Deeper-upstream emissions are not charged to the first-tier supplier, because who controls those emissions — and how finance would travel across tiers — is not observed.

This research preview combines published sector-level production accounts with transparent proportional allocation, an all-tier input–output multiplier and user-defined financing scenarios. It is intended for policy screening, research exploration and teaching. It is not a firm-level carbon inventory, a credit assessment, a causal model or an investment recommendation. First-tier direct exposure and all-tier embodied exposure are shown as distinct measures.
